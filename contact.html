Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What information do you need for a quote?

Pickup and drop-off locations, pickup times, number of passengers, your vehicle choice, and any special preferences or requirements.

Is there a minimum booking time?

The Cadillac Escalade and Lincoln Navigator have no minimum. The BMW Stretch Limousine, Mercedes Sprinter Limousine, Executive Sprinter, Transit Van and Sprinter Passenger Van have a 4-hour minimum.

How much wait time is included?

All pickups include 5–10 minutes of complimentary wait time. Airport pickups include 30 minutes, or 60 minutes for international flights.

What if my flight is early or delayed?

We track your flight and adjust your pickup time for early or late arrivals — no need to call us.

Do you provide child seats?

Five-point harness and booster seats may be available. Please ask about availability when you book.

When can I reach you?

Any time. Our reservation specialists are available 24/7 by phone, text or email.
