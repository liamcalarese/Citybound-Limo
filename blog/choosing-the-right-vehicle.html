How to Choose the Right Vehicle for Your Group

October 28, 20262 min readBy Citybound Limo

The right vehicle makes the trip. Too small and everyone's cramped; too big and you're paying for seats you don't need. Here's a quick guide to our fleet, organized by group size.

Small groups (up to 6)

Cadillac Escalade and Lincoln Navigator are executive SUVs that seat 5–6 passengers. Neither has a minimum booking time, so they're ideal for:

  • Airport transfers (the Escalade has full luggage space)
  • Business meetings and client pickups
  • Date nights and dinner reservations

Medium groups (6–11)

The BMW Stretch Limousine seats 6–9 and is the classic choice for weddings, proms and anniversaries. Luggage space is limited, so it's best for events rather than airport runs.

The Executive Sprinter seats 8–11 and offers a restroom option, which makes it great for longer trips like wine tours or mountain getaways.

Large groups (12–14)

  • Mercedes Sprinter Limousine (12–14 passengers) is our party favorite, built for bachelor and bachelorette parties, birthdays and nights out.
  • Sprinter Passenger Van (13–14 passengers) has luggage space, so it's perfect for group airport transfers.
  • Transit Van (13–14 passengers) is practical group transportation for events, sports and outings.

Our limousines, Sprinters and vans have a 4-hour minimum booking.

Still not sure? Call or text 980-358-4346 with your group size and plans, and we'll recommend the best fit.
