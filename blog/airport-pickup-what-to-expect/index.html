What to Expect From Your Airport Pickup

October 14, 20262 min readBy Citybound Limo

Air travel is stressful enough. Our airport service is designed so that the ride on either end is the easiest part of your trip. Here's what happens when you book an airport pickup or drop-off with Citybound Limo.

We track your flight

Once you've booked, we monitor your flight. If you land early, your chauffeur is already on the way. If you're delayed, we adjust your pickup time automatically, so there's no need to call us from the tarmac.

Meet & greet at baggage claim

For arrivals, your driver meets you at baggage claim, so you won't be hunting for your car at the curb. For departures, your driver can park and walk you to the check-in counter.

Generous complimentary wait time

Flights run late, bags take a while, and customs lines can be long. That's why every airport pickup includes:

  • 30 minutes of complimentary wait time for domestic flights
  • 60 minutes for international flights

(Standard non-airport pickups include 5–10 minutes.)

Choosing the right vehicle

If you're traveling with luggage, vehicle choice matters. The Cadillac Escalade seats up to 6 and has dedicated luggage space, so it's our go-to for most airport runs. Larger groups with bags should choose the Sprinter Passenger Van, which seats up to 14 and also has room for luggage. See the full list on our fleet page.

Tip: When you book, include your flight number and airline. It lets us track your arrival precisely.

Traveling with kids?

Five-point harness and booster seats may be available. Just ask when you book so we can confirm.

Ready to book? Reserve your airport transfer online, or call or text us at 980-358-4346. We're available 24/7.
