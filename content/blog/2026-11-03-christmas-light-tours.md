The holiday season is almost here, and so are Citybound Limo's **Christmas Light Tours**. Gather the family, your friends or your office crew, and see the season's brightest displays without fighting traffic or hunting for parking.

## Why take a light tour in a limo?

- **Everyone rides together.** Nobody has to stay home as the designated driver.
- **No parking hassles.** Sit back and enjoy the lights while we handle the driving.
- **It's an event in itself.** Holiday music and great company make the ride part of the fun.

## Picking a vehicle

For a family or a small group of friends, an **Escalade** or **Navigator** (up to 6) works perfectly. For bigger groups, the **BMW Stretch Limousine** (up to 9) or the **Mercedes Sprinter Limousine** (up to 14) turns the night into a party.

## Planning tips

1. **Book early.** Weekend evenings in December fill up fast.
2. **Go after dark.** Most displays look their best from about 6 PM onward.
3. **Tell us your must-sees.** If there's a neighborhood or display you love, let us know when you book.
